slotone

One operator · one day-book · offline

Your day, laid out in clean slots.

slotone is an appointment day-book for a business of one — a barber, a tutor, a mechanic, a therapist. Set your services and hours; it computes an honest, no-overlap slot grid, and every booking lives on your device. No accounts, no network.

A plain note: this is a single-device owner tool. Clients can’t self-book over the internet — that needs a server this deliberately doesn’t have. The Share button makes a read-only snapshot of your free times instead.

Single-device owner tool, not a booking service. slotone stores your day-book only in this browser and makes no network requests. It cannot let clients self-book, send reminders, or sync across devices. Keep your own CSV backups — clearing browser data erases everything.
